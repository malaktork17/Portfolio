# My First Portfolio Prototype 🌱

## About the Project
This is my **very first portfolio project**, created during my early high school years (Grade 10).  
It represents my first steps in web design and personal branding. The project may look simple and unpolished — but for me, it’s a milestone that marks the beginning of my journey.  

I decided to keep it public because I’m proud of every stage of my growth, even the imperfect ones. 🚀

---

## Vision at That Time
Back then, my goal was just to **start building something** — a space that reflects my skills, creativity, and passions.  
It wasn’t about perfection, it was about *taking the first step*.  

---

## Features (as they were)
- 🏠 Home page with a welcome message.  
- 📖 Sections about my **skills, portfolio, and hobbies**.  
- ✍️ Early attempts at combining design + storytelling.  
- 🌍 A mix of English/Arabic text to reflect my vision and identity.  

---

## Why It Matters
This project is not a polished portfolio.  
Instead, it’s a **snapshot of my learning journey** at age 16 — a reminder that progress comes from starting small and improving over time.  

Keeping it online shows my evaluators (and myself) how far I’ve come since those first attempts. 💡

---

## What’s Next
- I’m now working on updated portfolio projects with stronger design, responsive layouts, and better structure.  
- But this prototype will always stay public as a record of my **early enthusiasm and courage to try**.
